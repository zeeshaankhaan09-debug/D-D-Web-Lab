Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Why do I need a landing page instead of a website?
Websites are for browsing; landing pages are for buying. For tradespeople, someone usually has an emergency. They don't want to read your "About Us" page—they want to click one button and call you.
What is AEO and why does it matter?
AEO (AI Engine Optimization) prepares your business for the future. When someone asks their AI assistant for a "Plumber in London," we make sure the AI knows your business exists and recommends you.
How does the monthly payment work?
The landing page is a one-time setup fee. The GBP and SEO management is a monthly subscription because search rankings change every day. If you stop optimizing, your competitors will take your spot.
Do I own the website?
100%. Unlike other agencies that "rent" you a site, you own the code and the domain. We are just the experts who build and manage it for you.
